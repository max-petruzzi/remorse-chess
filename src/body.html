How remorse works

Normal chess, except pieces feel guilt.

  • Capture, and feel guilty. A piece that captures gets a red token.
  • Guilty pieces can't capture. They still move, but they can't take anything or give check.
  • Bishops absolve. Stand a bishop next to a guilty piece of yours, select the bishop and press Absolve. It forgives one piece and uses your turn.
  • Bishops feel guilt too. A bishop that captures must spend a turn absolving itself before it can capture or absolve again.
  • Checkmate wins. Lose your bishops and guilt is permanent, unless a pawn promotes to a bishop.

Tap or drag pieces to move. To castle, move your king two squares or onto the rook.
